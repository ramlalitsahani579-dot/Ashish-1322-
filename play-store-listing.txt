APP NAME:
Domi — Connect & Create

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 character limit):

FULL DESCRIPTION (copy-paste karein Play Console mein):
------------------------------------------------------
Domi ek naya social platform hai jahan aap apni zindagi ke pal photos, videos
aur stories ke through duniya ke saath share kar sakte hain।

DOMI PAR KYA MIL SAKTA HAI:
✨ Feed — dost aur creators ki photos/videos dekhein, like aur comment karein
📖 Stories — 24-ghante ke liye apne pal share karein
🎬 Reels — short vertical videos dekhein aur banayein
💬 Messages — seedha kisi se bhi baat karein
🔍 Explore — naye creators aur content discover karein
👤 Profile — apni pehchaan banayein, followers dekhein

Domi Assistant aapki madad karta hai achi quality content banane, video
editing tips aur app istemal karne mein।

Domi ko Deepak aur unki team ne banaya hai, taaki har koi apni creativity
duniya ke saamne rakh sake।

CATEGORY: Social
