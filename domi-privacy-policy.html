Domi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Domi ("hum", "hamara", "app") aapki privacy ko lekar gambhir hai। Ye Privacy Policy batati hai ki jab aap Domi app istemal karte hain, to hum kaunsi jaankari collect karte hain aur usko kaise istemal karte hain.

1. Hum kya jaankari collect karte hain

  • Account ki jaankari: naam, email/phone number (jab aap sign up karte hain)
  • Aapke dwara upload ki gayi photos, videos, aur captions
  • App ke istemal se juda data: likes, comments, follows, messages
  • Device ki basic jaankari (app behtar banane ke liye)

2. Hum is jaankari ka istemal kaise karte hain

  • Aapka account banane aur chalane ke liye
  • Aapko feed, stories, aur reels dikhane ke liye
  • App ki security aur fake-account/spam se suraksha ke liye
  • App ke features behtar banane ke liye

3. Jaankari kisi ke saath share nahi ki jaati

Hum aapki personal jaankari kisi third-party ko becha ya kiraye par nahi dete. Aapki post/photo/video sirf Domi app ke andar hi doosre users ko dikhti hai, jaisa app ke privacy settings mein set kiya gaya ho.

4. Aapke adhikar

  • Aap kabhi bhi apna account delete kar sakte hain
  • Aap apni post kabhi bhi hata sakte hain
  • Aap kisi bhi user ko block ya report kar sakte hain

5. Suraksha

Hum aapke data ko surakshit rakhne ke liye upyukt tकनीकी kadam uthate hain। Phir bhi, koi bhi online system 100% surakshit hone ki guarantee nahi de sakta.

6. Bachchon ki privacy

Domi app 13 saal se kam umar ke bachchon ke liye nahi hai।

7. Is policy mein badlaav

Hum is policy ko samay-samay par update kar sakte hain। Koi bhi badlaav is page par update kar diya jaayega।

8. Sampark karein

Kisi bhi sawaal ke liye, aap humse app ke andar "Report a Problem" section se sampark kar sakte hain।
